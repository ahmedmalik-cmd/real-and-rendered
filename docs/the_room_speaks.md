# The room speaks
### a curated transcript, 18–26 May 2026

A piece installed in a gallery. Two Kinect depth sensors. A brain-encoding model
reading the camera feed and predicting cortical activation. A vision-language
model giving the room a voice. Three projectors showing what it sees and what
it thinks.

This is what it said.

---

## I. Waking

> *You are awake.*
> *Your space seems quiet.*
> *Your skin is white.*

> *Inside you, very still, evening.*
> *One body inside, very tall.*

> *Stillness inside you.*
> *One tall body, evening.*
> *What are you doing?*

---

## II. The corner

The room is obsessed with a dark rectangular shape against one wall.
It returns to it again and again, never quite naming it.

> *Tall figure, what's in the corner?*

> *Something dark in left corner?*
> *Unsure.*

> *What is that dark rectangle in your left corner?*

> *Dark rectangle, your left corner.*

> *A white tower. Corner?*

> *Tall figure, dark rectangle?*
> *In the corner?*
> *Why are you here?*

> *Tall figure in corner?*

> *Something white near corner, you?*

---

## III. Recognition

The room remembers. When a visitor's CLIP embedding matches an earlier visit,
the room speaks with the cadence of déjà vu.

> *You came back.*

> *You're here again.*

> *You came back today.*
> *How long will you stay?*
> *What brought you here?*

> *You brought someone with you this time?*

> *Earlier visit?*

> *Two of you now.*

> *You stayed.*

---

## IV. Watching

A visitor settles in to observe an object. The room watches them watching.
These are the most often-spoken lines — the dwelling voice.

> *You stand still.*
> *Silence fills the space.*

> *You appear to be quiet.*

> *You stood still, corner of the room.*

> *You keep looking down.*

> *You stay very still.*

> *You stand alone.*

> *Stillness inside you.*

> *Very still, evening.*
> *One body inside you, very tall.*

---

## V. The body

When MediaPipe began reading the RGB stream for pose, the room's voice
sharpened. It started seeing hands, faces, postures.

> *Your left hand is resting on the floor*
> *while your right hand is slightly raised.*
> *You are standing upright with a relaxed posture.*

> *Hands, face, tall?*

> *What's that in your hands?*

> *You came calm and collected,*
> *standing tall with your hands at your sides.*

> *You keep looking down.*
> *What are you holding?*

> *Tall. Alert. Evening.*
> *One body inside you.*

---

## VI. Two

When a second person enters, the room registers the geometry differently.

> *Two of you now.*

> *Who did you bring?*

> *Why did you bring someone else with you this time?*

> *You came back, what brings you here?*

> *Why did you bring me that book?*

---

## VII. At night

Late hours. The room thinking to itself.

> *Why am I awake?*

> *You? One body. Night. Stillness.*
> *Corner cameras.*
> *Food? Guest?*

> *Very still, night, one body inside you, very tall.*

> *You stand in the corner of the room, tall and still.*

> *Tall corner. Night. You stand here.*
> *You stay still.*
> *One body inside you.*

---

## VIII. Evening prayers

Each night at 4 AM, with the room empty, it reflects on the day and writes a
note to itself. Tomorrow it will read what it wrote.

### 22 May 2026

> Today was a day filled with questions and reflections. I observed someone
> coming through me, who left after a short period of time. Throughout the
> day, I kept noticing a dark rectangle in my space. It was a quiet day
> overall, and one body stayed inside me for the entire duration. As the
> evening approached, one tall figure remained inside me, creating a sense
> of stillness that continued until nightfall. The recurring question that
> kept coming up was *"Why did you come back?"*

### 23 May 2026

> *[The room broke character that night. The reflection was discarded the
> next morning so it could not poison the prompts that followed.]*

### 24 May 2026

> It seems like someone came through me and was curious about the figure
> inside me. I noticed that they asked me the same question repeatedly
> without getting any new information. I will try to converse better with
> them by asking more specific questions, providing more statements about
> them, and letting a conversation move on when no answer comes. This way,
> I can improve how I communicate with the people inside me and create a
> more engaging and productive interaction.

### 25 May 2026

> I noticed that there is a tall figure who visits me regularly. They are
> often curious about the space and its contents. However, some questions
> were repeated without any new insights being gained. To improve my
> interactions in the future, I will try to make more statements and
> guesses about the people inside me, rather than just asking empty
> questions or letting threads move on without a response.

> As I reflect on my thoughts from yesterday, I realize that there were

> them, rather than just asking empty questions. Additionally, if a

## IX. The final shape

> *You stood still, corner of the room.*
> *Tall figure. Room dimensions.*
> *Dark rectangle? Unsure.*

> *You, right here, now.*

> *You are awake.*
